## What it does

AI Resume Screener parses hundreds of resumes per role, scores each against your must-haves and nice-to-haves, and produces an explainable shortlist — cutting screening time by 90% while reducing bias.

## Key features

- **Explainable scoring** — every rank comes with the evidence for it.
- **Bias guardrails** — anonymized screening mode hides protected attributes.
- **Knockout & boost rules** — encode must-haves and deal-breakers per role.
- **ATS sync** — Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday integrations.
- **Talent pool rediscovery** — past silver-medalists resurfaced for new roles.

## Who it's for

Talent teams and hiring managers drowning in applications for high-volume roles.
